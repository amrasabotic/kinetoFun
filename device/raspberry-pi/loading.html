Set up Wi-Fi

Use your phone to connect this box to your home Wi-Fi.

  1. 1Point your phone camera at the code and join the network it shows.
  2. 2A setup page opens. If it doesn't, open 192.168.4.1 in your phone's browser.
  3. 3Choose your home Wi-Fi, enter its password and tap Connect. Then watch this screen.

Wi-Fi stops working later? Unplug the box and plug it back in.
