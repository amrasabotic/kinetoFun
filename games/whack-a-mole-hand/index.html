WHACK-A-MOLE

How to play

Moles pop out of the holes. Move your open hand to aim the hammer, then close it into a fist to smash!

Golden moles are worth more, hard hats need two smashes, and never hit a bomb. Score enough points to pass each of the 10 levels.

Rest your hand on a big button to press it.
