FIRE BRIGADE

How to play

Fires break out in the building. Point your hand at a burning room to aim the hose. Open your hand to spray, make a fist to stop and refill the tank.

Pets wave from windows. Put out their fire, then hold the hose on the pet until the ladder brings it down.

Two players each get a fire engine: the left hand on screen drives the left one. Rest your hand on a button to press it.
