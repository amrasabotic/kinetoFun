How to Play

✋
Move your hand
Raise your hand to the camera. The circle on screen follows your fingertip.
⏳
Hold to place
Rest the circle on an empty square until its ring fills (a pinch works too).
🤖
1 or 2 players
Play X against the computer, or take turns with a friend: X first, then O.
🏆
Win
Get three in a row — across, down or diagonal. A full board is a draw.
🔁
Next round
After a game, hold on "Play again" to start a new one.
📊
Score
Wins for X and O are counted at the top of the screen.
