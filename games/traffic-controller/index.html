Traffic Controller

How to play

You run the lights at a busy crossroads. Cars queue on all four roads, and only one road can have a green light at a time.

Move your open hand to point at a road, then make a fist to give that road the green light. Make a fist on the WALK button to let people cross.

Don't let queues grow too long, and don't keep drivers waiting too long or they run the red! Rest your hand on a sign to press it.
