Pocket Pal Pet Show

Train your pet. Win the shows!

Train your pet's four skills with hand games, then enter shows against rival pets. Win rosettes and trophies, all the way to Grand Champion.

  • Sprint: pump your hand up and down fast
  • Hurdles: raise your hand to jump
  • Dance: move your hand to the lit pad on the beat
  • Memory: rest your hand on the pads in order

Every game has 3 levels. Win 2 stars to unlock the next, harder level.
