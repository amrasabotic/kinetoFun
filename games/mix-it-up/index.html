Mix It Up
The Potion Shop

How to play

Customers come to your shop and ask for a potion of a certain colour. Mix it from the colour jars and hand it over before they get tired of waiting.

Open hand to move. Make a fist over a jar to scoop, open your hand over the pot to drop it in. Fist over the pot fills a bottle; open over the customer to serve it.

Red and yellow make orange, yellow and blue make green... Rest your hand on a sign to press it.
